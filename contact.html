Contact and Support

Need help with your Business Web Center workspace? Start with your business owner or the administrator who invited you. They can manage staff access, branches, and most workspace settings.

For account support: Use the support contact supplied during your account setup. Include your business name, the email address on the account, a short description of the issue, and a screenshot when possible.

What to include in a support request

  • Your business and branch name
  • The email address used to sign in
  • The page or feature affected
  • What you expected, what happened, and when
  • A screenshot that does not expose passwords or confidential client details

Urgent access or data concerns

Contact your workspace owner immediately to suspend a former staff member, reset access, or report a suspected data issue.
