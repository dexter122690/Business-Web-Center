Itemized weekly painter job amounts

Deploy 089 after 088, before publishing frontend version 20261125-job-items.
Applied and verified in BWC production on November 25, 2026. Workspace and
stored-file hashes matched before/after; the new save validation is active.
This migration changes validation only, not payroll data or access permissions.

Behavior
- Each unit has job descriptions with an amount for each job and a + Job button.
- Amount this week is read-only and automatically sums those amounts in centavos.
- Up to 30 job lines per unit; descriptions are required beside nonzero amounts.
- Agreed labor stays separate and cross-date overpayment guards still apply.
- Each week's itemization is saved with its worksheet. Next-week descriptions
  carry forward with zero amounts; staff must enter the next week's payment.
- Legacy free text and its saved weekly total remain a single unitemized line
  until edited. Prices are never guessed or parsed from old descriptions.
- Save updates one working worksheet; released copies/PDFs remain immutable.
- Print and PDF show each weekly job description and amount. The retained-units
  section remains a balance reference, not a second list of weekly payments.
- Older clients cannot silently remove an existing worksheet's itemized lines.
- Other BWC modules and employee profile data are unchanged.
