082 - Branch-scoped employee management

Applied to the BWC database on 2026-11-23.

The migration creates a separate employee-management permission for an existing
payroll editor. It does not grant anyone access automatically or change any other
BWC module. The exact owner-approved account grant was applied separately and
recorded in payroll_v2_events. Account-specific maintenance SQL is kept outside
the website repository.

Employee managers can add/edit/archive/restore branch employee profiles and
access their private details and supporting photos. Payroll rules, permission
settings, released-payslip corrections and the full-backup UI remain owner-only.
Removing or downgrading payroll access also removes employee-management access.
Changes force already-open payroll worksheets to reload before saving, so a
previously redacted profile cannot overwrite private details.

Deployment: push the accompanying website commit. The database update and
approved grant are already applied. Users must refresh/reopen Payroll after
the website deployment to see the new buttons. Owners can later revoke this
permission in Payroll access using Add & manage employees.

Verification: full automated suite passed (270 tests), including SQL permission,
private-file, stale-save, branch-isolation and released-history checks. Live
verification confirmed the exact grant, no other management grants, and matching
checksums for payroll state, profiles, files, roles and other permissions.
