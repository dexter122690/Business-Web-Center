PMS payment-based schedule - 2026-11-25

Applied 090_pms_payment_schedule.sql to the BWC Supabase project successfully.
Deploy the matching frontend commit through the normal Push origin workflow.

Behavior
- Assigned technician is free text, not a payroll employee dropdown.
- Both odometer readings are required for new/edited PMS invoices; zero is valid.
- Next PMS date is read-only: six calendar months after dated payments first
  cover the invoice total. Month-end dates clamp to the last valid day.
- Partial payment or insufficient dated payment history leaves the date pending.
- Later payments, payment corrections and invoice total edits recalculate it.
- Invoice lines and PMS details save together in one transaction.
- New offline PMS requests use the same validation. Existing immutable outbox
  requests/backups are not rewritten or deleted. An old queued PMS request with
  missing odometers will remain queued for owner review instead of saving an
  incomplete record. Keep its recovery backup; do not recreate it blindly.

Recovery
The private backup stores original dates only; it is not an invoice/payment
backup. Restoring a manual date requires deliberately revising the date guard
as well. Do not disable these rules or change financial records just to restore
an old manually entered schedule.
