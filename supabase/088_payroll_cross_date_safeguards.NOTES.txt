Payroll cross-date safeguards

Deploy 088 after 087, before publishing frontend version 20261125-cross-date.
The migration only adds private validation helpers and updates existing guards.
It does not rewrite payroll records, files, employee profiles, roles or other tabs.
The BWC production migration was applied and verified on November 25, 2026.
Before/after workspace and file hashes matched; the save guard is active.

Behavior
- One stable employee/job ID connects payments in all saved weeks.
- Total weekly payments, prior settlements, uncovered legacy payments and received
  retention cannot exceed a confirmed agreed labor amount.
- A past payment updates later UNRELEASED worksheet balances, not weekly pay.
- Released sources/PDFs, their prior-payment history and retention receipts stay locked.
- Choose Use existing job when adding a known unit to an earlier saved period.
  New entries with the exact same invoice and plate are rejected as duplicates.
  Unit/model name alone is not an identity match. Missing identifiers need review.
- Confirmed agreed labor must match across known-price weeks. Unknown historical
  contracts remain pending; they are not silently assigned a zero or guessed price.
- A zero pre-BWC anchor can move earlier for a linked job only when no released
  payslip or received retention locks it. Nonzero opening settlements stay fixed.
- The database independently rejects overpayments and stale later balances;
  older frontend clients do not silently bypass these checks.

Verification

  Nov 10 entry 6,000 rejected; 3,000 accepted; later opening 7,000 / balance 2,000.
- Browser verified failed save preserves input and valid save enables Print/PDF.
- Both half-short payslip pages rendered and visually checked using fictional data.
- No fictional/test records were inserted into the live database.
