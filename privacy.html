Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 4, 2026

Business Web Center provides business-management tools for approved customer workspaces. This policy explains how the platform handles account and business data.

Information we store

We store the information needed to operate your workspace, such as account details, business and branch details, invoices, expenses, inventory, payroll records, schedules, and customer feedback.

How information is used

Your data is used to provide the services you request, keep records connected across your workspace, protect accounts, provide customer support, and improve reliability.

Access and separation

Business records are separated by customer workspace and branch. Authorized staff may access only the business areas assigned to them. Platform administrators may access records only when needed for support, security, account administration, or legal obligations.

Security

We use authenticated access and database access controls designed to limit access to authorized users. No online service can promise absolute security, so account owners should use strong passwords and remove access for former staff promptly.

Retention and deletion

Records remain available while a workspace is active, subject to operational and legal requirements. Account owners can request support for account or data deletion. Deleting a business transaction may affect connected reports and balances.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact the Business Web Center administrator using the support details provided during your account setup.
